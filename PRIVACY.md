# Privacy Policy — Sudoku: Scan, Solve, Learn

_Last updated: 27/07/2026_

**Short version: this app collects nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere.**

**No data collection.** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
tracking of any kind.

**No network access.** The app contains no networking code. It cannot send data off your
device because it has no means to do so, and it works fully offline.

**Camera and photos.** If you use the Scan feature, the app reads the photo you take or
choose so it can recognise the digits in a sudoku grid. This happens entirely on your device
using a built-in machine-learning model. Your photo is never uploaded, never stored by us,
and is discarded once the grid has been read.

**What is stored on your device.** Your chosen colour theme and your play statistics (puzzles
solved, hints used, and similar counters) are saved locally on your device only. Deleting the
app removes them permanently.

**Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone,
including children.

**Changes.** Any future change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated
date.
